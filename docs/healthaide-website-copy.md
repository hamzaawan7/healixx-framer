## 1) Brand & Voice Guidelines

### 1.1 Brand one‑liner
Healthaide helps people turn everyday health signals into clearer, more confident wellness decisions—through a modern, simple experience built for trust.

### 1.2 Tone attributes
- **Professional:** clear, credible, and calm.
- **Human:** supportive, non‑judgmental language.
- **Modern:** concise, product-led statements.
- **Trustworthy:** privacy-first framing, no hype.

### 1.3 Writing principles
- **Be specific:** explain what the user can do and what changes.
- **Be short:** prefer simple sentences.
- **Be action‑oriented:** every section ends with a clear next step.
- **Avoid medical promises:** use safe language (“can help”, “designed to”, “supports”).
